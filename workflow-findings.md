# Workflow Findings

## Objective
Evaluate how generative AI can assist with knowledge tasks such as research synthesis and documentation drafting.

## Observations
- AI significantly reduces time spent on initial drafts.
- Structured prompts improve clarity and response consistency.
- AI outputs require human validation for accuracy.

## Productivity Impact
Estimated 30–40% reduction in time spent on planning and drafting tasks during testing.

## Conclusion
AI shows strong potential as a knowledge assistant for professionals working in research-heavy environments.
